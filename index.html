October CPI runs hotter than expected on both headline and core prices

▼ Bearish, high impact

Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast. Core prices rose 0.3% against a 0.2% expectation, the third straight month at that pace. The BLS published no 12-month rate because October 2025 prices were not collected during last year's shutdown; the last annual reading, for September, was 3.7%.

Why it matters: Core inflation running above forecast keeps the Fed on a tightening path, which raises discount rates on high-valuation stocks and weighs on the broader market.

  • S&P 500
  • Nasdaq Composite
  • rate-sensitive growth stocks
  • homebuilders

Treasury yields climb toward 5% as traders price a December Fed hike

▼ Bearish, high impact

The 10-year yield rose to about 4.99% and the 2-year to about 4.69% after the CPI report. Fed funds futures moved toward pricing a near certain rate hike at the December 8 to 9 meeting. Governor Waller leans toward a quarter point hike and said a half point should be considered if core CPI came in around 0.3% or higher.

  • S&P 500
  • Nasdaq Composite
  • banks
  • mortgage rates
  • 10-year Treasury
Why it matters

Why it matters: A shift from expected cuts to expected hikes raises borrowing costs across the economy and pulls money away from stocks toward bonds, which pressures valuations.

SourcesFXStreetStockAnalysis

S&P 500, Dow and Nasdaq close lower on Nov 10 after inflation data

▼ Bearish, medium impact

The S&P 500 fell 0.4% to 7,520.93, the Dow lost 0.3% to 52,231.89 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.7% to 25,065.87. Traders raised their forecasts for Fed rate hikes after the inflation report, and Treasury yields jumped even as oil kept easing.

  • S&P 500
  • Dow Jones Industrial Average
  • Nasdaq Composite
Why it matters

Why it matters: The Nasdaq fell furthest because long-duration tech shares are the most sensitive to higher yields, so the index losses reflect rate pressure more than company news.

SourcesLa Nacion

▼ Bearish, medium impact

Gold lost about 1.3% on the day and fell toward the 4,030 to 4,060 range. Bitcoin dropped toward 73,000 after the hot CPI print. Both moves came as the dollar strengthened and Treasury yields rose.

Oil keeps easing on reports of possible US-Iran talks

▲ Bullish, medium impact

Crude prices fell further on Nov 10 as concern eased about a possible US military strike on Iran. Reports of a disputed Iran deal were cited as a driver of the drop in the days before. WTI was near 87.58 on Nov 9, according to an approximate market summary.

  • energy stocks
  • airlines
  • transport
  • consumer spending
Why it matters

Why it matters: Lower oil reduces the energy part of inflation and cuts input costs, which helps the case that inflation can cool. Oil is still high, so the offset is modest.
