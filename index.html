Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang steps down as board probes reports of a foreign intelligence effort; earnings due Tuesday

▼ Bearish, high impact

Nvidia's board opened an internal investigation into CEO Jensen Huang, and Huang has stepped down. Colette Kress, the longtime CFO, is interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent replacement. Nvidia shares fell again on Friday and more than $600 billion of market value was wiped out that week. Nvidia reports quarterly results Tuesday after the close, its first report without Huang.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the largest weight in the AI trade, so its leadership turmoil and any guidance change on Tuesday can move the whole tech sector and the Nasdaq. Consensus expects about $2.49 EPS and roughly $108.8 billion in revenue, so a miss or cautious outlook would add pressure.

Hot October CPI keeps inflation in focus and pushes Treasury yields higher

▼ Bearish, high impact

The October consumer price index rose 0.4% month over month, above the 0.3% forecast, after a 0.6% gain in September. Core CPI rose 0.3% against a 0.2% forecast, the third straight monthly gain of that size. Average hourly earnings were up 3.1% from a year earlier, below the roughly 4% inflation pace. Yields rose after the report, with the 10-year near 5.15% and the 2-year at 4.87% on Friday.

  • regional banks
  • S&P 500
Why it matters

Why it matters: Sticky core inflation keeps the Federal Reserve from cutting and raises the chance of a hike, which lifts borrowing costs and compresses valuations for high-multiple growth stocks.
