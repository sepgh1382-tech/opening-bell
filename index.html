Nvidia plunges 14% after reports of an SEC accounting review

▼ Bearish, high impact

Reuters reported that SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia, and California's attorney general sent the company an investigative subpoena. Nvidia closed at $125.53, a 52-week low, and chip peers such as Micron and AMD fell more than 12%.

Why it matters: Nvidia is the largest driver of AI-related index weight, so a sharp drop in the leading chip name pulls down the whole AI trade and the indexes that hold it.

  • semiconductors
  • Micron
  • AMD
  • Nasdaq 100

S&P 500 enters a correction as the AI selloff spreads

▼ Bearish, high impact

The S&P 500 closed at 6,910.39, down 3.70%, its seventh straight loss and an 8.6% drop for the week, its worst since April 2025. The Dow and S&P 500 are now more than 10% below their record highs, and the VIX closed at 35.89, its first close above 30 since late March.

  • S&P 500
  • Dow Jones
  • Nasdaq Composite
  • technology sector
  • VIX
Why it matters

Why it matters: Technology fell 7.27% and was the worst sector. Consumer staples were the only sector to finish higher, a sign of defensive rotation rather than a full exit from stocks. Taiwan's TAIEX fell 6.23%, a record weekly loss, which shows the chip selloff is global.

SourcesCappnotesThe Wall Street Republic

Fed hawks push for more rate hikes, and markets lean toward a December increase

▼ Bearish, high impact

St. Louis Fed President Musalem said the US likely needs several rate hikes by spring to control inflation, and called the current 3.75% to 4.00% rate accommodative. Kalshi prediction markets priced a 25 basis point hike at the December meeting as the most likely outcome, though the snapshots they showed ranged from 77% to 91%.

  • S&P 500
  • rate-sensitive growth stocks
  • banks
  • Treasuries
Why it matters

Why it matters: Higher expected rates raise borrowing costs and lower the value of future earnings, which weighs most on high-valuation growth stocks. A hike bet also removes the hope of a Fed rescue during the selloff.

Treasury yields fall sharply as investors buy safe assets

Neutral, medium impact

The 10-year Treasury yield fell about 12 basis points to 4.92%, and the 2-year fell 13 basis points to 4.63%. Bond prices rose as stocks sold off.

  • Treasuries
  • banks
  • utilities
  • REITs
Why it matters

Why it matters: Lower yields ease pressure on stock valuations. The move came from risk-off buying rather than a softer Fed, so it reads as a flight to safety, not a sign that rate hikes are off the table.

Global Payments jumps 10% on a reported takeover approach

▲ Bullish, low impact

Bloomberg reported that private equity firm GTCR is exploring a takeover of Global Payments worth about $50 billion including debt. The stock rose 9.95% on the news.

  • Global Payments (GPN)
  • payments sector
Why it matters

Why it matters: Buyout interest in a beaten-down company suggests some private buyers still see value in lower-priced stocks. It is a single-stock story, so its effect on the broader market is limited.

Bottom line

The AI and chip trade is driving a broad selloff, and the S&P 500 is now more than 10% below its record. Rising rate-hike expectations remove the usual hope of a Fed rescue, and a VIX above 35 signals real fear. Defensive stocks held up, so investors are rotating rather than leaving equities entirely. The next test is whether the Nvidia probe deepens and whether the selling steadies before the holiday-shortened week.
