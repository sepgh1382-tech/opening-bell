Hot October CPI lifts odds of a December Fed rate hike

▼ Bearish, high impact

Consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, and core CPI rose 0.3% against 0.2% expected. The 2-year Treasury yield jumped to 4.69% and S&P 500 futures fell 0.6% on the morning of the release.

Why it matters: Before the report, markets already priced a better than 80 percent chance of a quarter-point hike at the Dec 8 to 9 meeting. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said a core reading around 0.3 percent or higher could justify considering a 50 basis point hike, so higher yields hit valuations.

  • S&P 500
  • Nasdaq 100
  • rate-sensitive growth stocks
  • banks

SourcesStockanalysisReuters via Khaleej Times

Nvidia shares fall 6.5% as AI selloff deepens

▼ Bearish, high impact

Nvidia fell 6.5% on Tuesday Nov 10 and was on track to lose more than $470 billion in market value over two sessions. Nasdaq Composite was down about 1% that afternoon. Nvidia also hired outside lawyers for an internal investigation involving CEO Jensen Huang.

  • NVDA
  • semiconductors
  • Nasdaq Composite
Why it matters

Why it matters: Nvidia is the largest AI bellwether, so its slide pulls the rest of the AI chain lower. The hot CPI print and the investigation headlines came on the same day, which made the drop worse.

SourcesThe Star (Reuters)IBTimes UK

Tech in correction after worst chip rout since 2020

▼ Bearish, high impact

On Thursday Nov 5 the S&P 500 tech sector fell 7.3% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor index fell more than 12%, both their biggest one-day drops in more than six years. The Nasdaq Composite closed nearly 12% below its Oct 6 all-time high, past the 10% mark that defines a correction.

  • Nasdaq Composite
  • semiconductors
  • AI infrastructure
  • Dell
  • CoreWeave
  • Nebius
Why it matters

Why it matters: Investors worry that Democratic midterm wins could slow the AI data center buildout. Truist strategist Keith Lerner called it a buyers' strike in tech, with tech lagging the S&P 500 by its widest margin since May 2002.

SourcesReuters via Khaleej TimesADN (AI stock wipeout coverage)

Data center equipment and neocloud stocks slide

▼ Bearish, medium impact

GE Vernova fell more than 6% after SoftBank revised its equipment procurement plans for US data center projects. Iren fell 10%, Nebius 7% and CoreWeave 3% on the same day. All three neocloud names are down at least 40% from the Nasdaq's Oct 6 high.

  • GE Vernova
  • Iren
  • Nebius
  • CoreWeave
  • Dell
  • Sandisk
Why it matters

Why it matters: Cuts to data center spending plans reduce demand for power equipment and cloud capacity, and they test how the AI buildout will be financed.

SourcesThe Star (Reuters)

Anthropic IPO could price before Thanksgiving, timing not confirmed

Neutral, medium impact

Reports say Anthropic is targeting a Nasdaq listing in mid to late November, possibly as early as the week of Nov 16, and that it publicly filed its S-1 on Oct 26. The company has not announced a date, and sources disagree on the timing.

Why it matters: A very large AI listing could either refresh interest in the sector or draw money away from existing AI names, so the timing matters for the tech tape next week.
