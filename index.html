AI and chip selloff deepens as Nvidia drops and futures slide

▼ Bearish, high impact

Nvidia fell about 6% on Tuesday after a sharp rally the session before, and chip stocks broadly dropped while the Nasdaq fell 1.48%. US index futures slid again on Wednesday as the AI chip selloff deepened.

Why it matters: Nvidia and the chip group carry a large share of index weight and of AI-driven earnings expectations, so a continued slide drags the Nasdaq and the broader S&P 500 lower.

Fed's preferred inflation gauge rises, keeping December hike talk alive

▼ Bearish, high impact

Wednesday's PCE inflation report showed the Fed's preferred gauge rising, and stocks fell that day alongside AI stocks. Oxford Economics had forecast headline PCE at 3.8% year on year for October, up from 3.6% in September.

  • S&P 500
  • Treasuries
  • rate-sensitive growth stocks
  • financials
Why it matters

Why it matters: A hotter inflation reading raises the odds that the Fed tightens again at its December 9 meeting, which lifts Treasury yields and pressures valuations. Sources disagree on the market-implied odds, ranging from about 59% to 85%, so the exact pricing is unclear.

SourcesFox BusinessOxford EconomicsInvestmentNews

Nvidia leadership change and muted earnings reaction weigh on AI sentiment

▼ Bearish, medium impact

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia's president, CEO and board member effective November 11, and CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia beat estimates in its fiscal Q3 report on November 17, but the shares barely reacted.

  • NVDA
  • AI infrastructure stocks
  • Nasdaq Composite
Why it matters

Why it matters: Leadership uncertainty at the largest AI chip company, together with a weak reaction to a strong beat, has made investors more cautious about AI spending and valuations.

Sharp one-session rebound follows the S&P 500's worst week since April 2025

Neutral, medium impact

On Monday the Dow rose about 1,448 points to 49,595.31, the S&P 500 gained 326.78 points to 7,237.17 and the Nasdaq Composite jumped about 1,472 points. The rebound came after the S&P 500 had its weakest week since April 2025 and finished the prior Friday more than 10% below its October record.

  • Dow Jones
  • S&P 500
  • Nasdaq Composite
Why it matters

Why it matters: The size of the bounce shows how volatile the market is right now. Traders are reading it as a relief move rather than a trend change, and the follow-through selling since then suggests the rebound has not held.

SourcesCNBCBenzinga
